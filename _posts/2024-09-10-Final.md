## Project One: EU-DEMO Power exhaust modelling

In this project, an EU-DEMO 2D power exhaust modelling case is provided. The deuterium, helium and argon are considered. The totol power entering the Scrape-Off Layer is 150MW. Please answer the following question according to the corespoing plots from the modelling results.<br>

**Question 1**: Above plot it this computational mesh used for the EU-DEMO power exhaust modelling. Please estimate the major radius and minius radius and explain the reason.<br>

**Question 2**: Above plot is the poloidal and toroidal magnetic field. If the Temperature is assumed to be 250ev, please estimated the power decay length ${\lambda}_{q}$ and explain the reason.<br>

**Question 3**: If we assume that most of the power transport from the upstream to the outer target, please estimated the power flux at the outer target without volumetric power dissipation and explain the reason. In order to avoid the power exhaust problem, how much power need to be dissipated.<br>

**Question 4**: The total power flux at the outer target include the plasma contribution and the surface recombination. In the following 'code' part, **ne3dr** is the electron density profiles, **te3dr** is the temperature profiles, **dsr** is the coordinates along the outer target. 1) Please calcualte and plot the total power flux **ft3dr** at the outer target. The scripts for plot is already provied in the following and you need to finish the calculation of **ft3dr**. 2) Explain whether the power exhaust problem is solved or not for this case. <br>

**Question 5** In the modelling results, the helium particles is considered. Explain why we consider the helium. We also consider the argon impurity, please explain why the Argon impurity is considered.<br>
